Nova Student Tech Day

One day for building, sharing and experimenting. Bring an idea and a laptop — leave with something that runs in a browser.

Save your place

Free for every student. No experience needed.

What happens on the day

Six short workshops, three student talks and one very loud demo hour. Everything is hands-on. Nothing is a lecture.

  • Build a page from an empty folder
  • Take apart a real website with developer tools
  • Show your work in the demo hour

Schedule

  1. 09:30 Doors open, find your team
  2. 10:00 Workshop one — your first web page
  3. 11:30 Workshop two — styling and layout
  4. 13:00 Lunch and student talks
  5. 14:30 Build time with mentors
  6. 16:00 Demo hour and closing

Three sessions to pick from

Code as a creative tool

Write a page that reacts to what you type. A gentle first look at making a browser do something on demand.

Room B2 · 45 minutes

Your first robot dashboard

Build the control panel for a robot that does not exist yet. Buttons, cards and a very optimistic status light.

Lab 1 · 60 minutes

Design jam: pick a palette

Three colours, one font stack, one hour. Learn why spacing does more work than decoration.

Studio · 45 minutes
